# Mobile games market 2025-2026: state, trends and positioning for an indie puzzle-roguelite (Wumpus Adventures)

Research date: 2026-10-12. All "2025" figures are actuals (reported Feb-Mar 2026). "2026" figures are forecasts unless noted. Many search summaries were secondary; where a figure comes from a vendor blog it is flagged.

## 1. Market size and growth (revenue, downloads, genre, platform, region)

### Takeaway
Mobile is a mature, revenue-flat/low-growth, download-shrinking market: Sensor Tower has 2025 mobile IAP at $81.75B (+1.3%) and downloads at 50.4B (-7.2%), while Newzoo's broader definition gives $113.3B (+10.7%). Puzzle is the #2 genre by IAP revenue and one of the fastest-growing; hybrid-casual is the only segment with meaningful growth. Growth is coming from monetizing existing players, not new installs.

### Cited Findings
- Sensor Tower State of Gaming 2026 (covers CY2025, published 2026-02-26): mobile game IAP revenue $81.75B (+1.3% YoY); mobile downloads 50.41B (-7.2%). Non-gaming app IAP was $85.6B (+21%), overtaking games. — [GameDev Reports summary of Sensor Tower](https://gamedevreports.substack.com/p/sensor-tower-state-of-gaming-2026); [Sensor Tower press release](https://sensortower.com/press/press-release-sensor-tower-state-of-gaming-gaming-drove-52-billion-downloads-82b-iap-revenue-on-mobile-and-12b-premium-revenue-on-steam)
- Sensor Tower methodology covers App Store and Google Play only; third-party Android stores excluded. — [Sensor Tower blog](https://sensortower.com/blog/state-of-gaming-2026) (via search summary)
- Newzoo: global games 2025 = $201.6B, first time above $200B; mobile = $113.3B (+10.7%), over half the total, despite falling downloads. Mobile forecast 2026 = $121.1B. Total market forecast 2028 about $234.4-234.5B. No mobile-only 2028 number found. — [PocketGamer.biz](https://www.pocketgamer.biz/global-games-revenue-surpasses-200bn-for-the-first-time-mobile-generates-113bn/); [GameDev Reports on Newzoo](https://gamedevreports.substack.com/p/newzoo-gaming-market-surpassed-200). Note: Newzoo's Sept 2025 forecast had mobile at $103.0B (+2.9%) and total $188.8B, so actuals were revised well above; Sensor Tower and Newzoo definitions differ (Newzoo includes China third-party Android, etc.) — [PocketGamer.biz](https://www.pocketgamer.biz/newzoo-global-games-market-set-to-hit-1888bn-in-2025)
- Platform split 2025 (Sensor Tower): App Store revenue $52.5B (+0.6%), 7.8B installs (-5.7%), ~15% of installs; Google Play revenue $30B (+2.8%), 42.4B installs (-7.3%), ~81% of installs. So iOS ≈ 64% of store revenue, Android ≈ 81% of installs. — [GameDev Reports](https://gamedevreports.substack.com/p/sensor-tower-state-of-gaming-2026)
- Genre revenue 2025 (Sensor Tower): strategy $20.2B (strongest growth), puzzle $14.4B; strategy and puzzle are the fastest-growing genres by revenue; RPG declining; shooters growing, especially Asia. Puzzle ≈ 18% of mobile IAP (my arithmetic). — [GameDev Reports](https://gamedevreports.substack.com/p/sensor-tower-state-of-gaming-2026)
- Conflicting puzzle figure: Naavik citing Sensor Tower says puzzle >$10B IAP (+14% YoY), 2nd after strategy, and ~9.7B downloads in 2025 (about 19% of installs); the 9.7B also matches the 2024 figure, so verify. — [Naavik](https://naavik.co/digest/what-leading-match-3-and-merge-games-do-differently/)
- Hybrid-casual IAP revenue +20% YoY, the only segment with meaningful revenue growth; hypercasual is the only segment with download growth (modest). Casual top-25 D7 retention has declined since early 2022; top hybrid-casual now beats casual on D7. — [GameDev Reports](https://gamedevreports.substack.com/p/sensor-tower-state-of-gaming-2026)
- Puzzle is the largest genre by ad revenue share (53% Feb-Apr 2026, 19 countries only). Mobile game ad revenue exceeded $12B in 2025. — [WN Hub](https://wnhub.io/news/analytics/item-51344)
- Regions 2025: Europe drove most revenue gains; US revenue nearly flat; North American publishers -$1.78B IAP vs Asian studios +$2.58B; LATAM downloads declining (previously cited as a growth driver); Middle East/Africa growing; China time spent declining. SEA not covered. — [GameDev Reports](https://gamedevreports.substack.com/p/sensor-tower-state-of-gaming-2026)
- Japan: puzzle >30% of mobile game downloads (Jun 2024-May 2025). — [Sensor Tower JP](https://sensortower.com/ja/blog/state-of-mobile-puzzle-games-JP) (via search summary)
- Steam premium revenue 2025 $11.7B (+13%), installs 857M (+6%); indie revenue on Steam declined in 2025 partly due to a classification effect (Black Myth: Wukong counted as indie in 2024). — [GameDev Reports](https://gamedevreports.substack.com/p/sensor-tower-state-of-gaming-2026)

### Inferences
- A solo dev is entering a flat-revenue, shrinking-install market; the pie grows via ARPU, so premium or low-volume high-ARPPU strategies fit better than volume UA.
- Android is the volume platform (81% of installs, and the likelier cheap-CPI/LATAM route) while iOS is the revenue platform (about 64% of store revenue). A LATAM-leaning audience skews Android and low-ARPU.
- Puzzle sits in the right half of the growth map; roguelike has no clean Sensor Tower mobile category figure (see gaps).

### Gaps
- No LATAM/SEA/Europe regional revenue totals or 2023-2024 comparison series found; the only Sensor Tower LATAM pages found were quarterly top-chart snippets (AI-generated posts, low reliability). No AppMagic, Statista, Business of Apps or data.ai numbers retrieved. No genre-split forecasts for 2026-2028. No roguelike/roguelite mobile genre revenue figure.

## 2. Genre trends (puzzle, casual, roguelite, daily puzzles, AI, retro)

### Takeaway
Within puzzle, growth in 2026 is in block/arrow, sort and other hybrid-casual puzzle hits with meta layers; match/merge is crowded and mature. Roguelite on mobile is dominated by action-survivor hits, and deckbuilder/puzzle-roguelites (Balatro, Slay the Spire) succeed mainly as premium ports. Evidence on cozy, retro and AI-content trends was thin.

### Cited Findings
- PocketGamer.biz (Aug 25, 2026, H1 2026 data from Azur Games): block puzzle (including arrow-style games) leads downloads and rose sharply Jan-summer 2026; Sort Puzzle out-earns Sort; physics puzzle audience shifting to block/sort; installs of top titles down YoY while revenue rises in most genres; new releases are 2% of the top-300 installs chart in 2026 (8% mid-2023), over half of the top 300 predate 2024. Roguelike not mentioned. — [PocketGamer.biz](https://www.pocketgamer.biz/puzzle-games-rise-as-mobile-genres-compete-for-attention-in-2026/)
- Hybrid-casual hits (e.g. Color Block Jam, Pixel Flow per one 2026 guide) are re-engineered hypercasual hooks with meta layer, IAP economy and seasonal pass; ARPDAU claimed 4-7x hypercasual (unverified, vendor-style source). — [Cinevva guide](https://app.cinevva.com/guides/casual-games-trends-2026); [GameGrowthAdvisor](https://gamegrowthadvisor.com/blog/2026-04-16-hybrid-casual-game-design-strategy-2026/)
- Balatro mobile (Sept 2024; iOS, Android, Apple Arcade) topped sales charts within hours and reportedly earned >$500K in 5 days; PC sales >2M. Slay the Spire 2 is still PC-only as of Sept 2026. No verified 2025-26 mobile sales for either. — [VGC](https://www.videogameschronicle.com/news/hit-deck-builder-balatro-is-now-available-for-mobile/); [WN Hub](https://wnhub.io/news/investment/item-45940); [Gurugamer](https://gurugamer.com/mobile-games/top-10-deck-building-games-to-play-on-mobile-2026-27352)
- Survivor.io (roguelike + hack-and-slash) reached large revenue (2024 piece); this is action, not puzzle. — via [Cinevva/search summary](https://app.cinevva.com/guides/casual-games-trends-2026)
- Daily puzzles: Connections became NYT's second most played game after Wordle shortly after its 2023 beta; NYT games archive subscription was $5/month or $40/year (older pricing). No reliable 2026 player counts found. — [TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2023/08/28/connections-is-the-new-york-times-most-played-game-after-wordle); [Fast Company](https://www.fastcompany.com/90938716/The%20quest%20to%20create%20the%20next%20Wordle)
- AI clones: after Freya Holmér's rotating-Tetris prototype (March 2026), up to four AI-generated mobile clones appeared within days; Witch Beam reported 80+ clones of Unpacking since 2021; copyright doesn't protect mechanics; "no generative AI" disclaimers emerging as a differentiator. — [Kotaku](https://kotaku.com/people-are-using-ai-to-quickly-clone-games-before-the-indie-devs-can-release-them-2000716336); [AI Weekly](https://aiweekly.co/alerts/ai-vibecoding-turns-indie-game-demos-into-clones-within-days); [Promise Legal](https://blog.promise.legal/ai-game-assets-copyright-steam-disclosure-2026/)
- Store volume: one vendor blog claims app submissions +60% in Q1 2026 vs Q1 2025 (unverified) and that charts favor established apps. — [Stora](https://stora.sh/blog/2026-05-09-app-store-discovery-ai-app-boom-survival-guide)

### Inferences
- A deduction/puzzle roguelite is differentiated from the crowded block/sort hybrid-casual hits, but cannot rely on that genre's tailwind; its closest proof point is Balatro-style premium, not F2P hybrid.
- Fast cloneability makes a distinctive hook, brand/character (Wumpus IP), and early community more defensible than mechanics alone.

### Gaps
- No data found on cozy games, retro pixel-art demand, AI-generated procedural content as a trend, merge/match saturation metrics (beyond Naavik), Wordle/NYT 2026 numbers, or Deconstructor of Fun analysis (searches returned nothing from it).

## 3. User acquisition environment

### Takeaway
UA is expensive and rising (US blended CPI about $4-5 in 2026 per vendor blogs; iOS several times Android), so paid UA is generally uneconomic for a solo premium/low-ARPU title; organic/short-form creator content and ASO are the realistic channels. Data quality on benchmarks is poor and sources conflict.

### Cited Findings
- Liftoff 2025 Casual Gaming report (Feb 2024-Feb 2025): median CPI $1.41 iOS vs $0.14 Android (casual); mid-core $0.73 Android / $3.65 iOS (12-month averages, secondhand). — [Statista page](https://www.statista.com/statistics/1241651/global-cpi-casual-gaming-apps-genre-platform); [FoxData](https://foxdata.com/en/blogs/2026-mobile-game-user-acquisition-cost-benchmarks-how-much-should-you-spend/) (secondary)
- FoxData (vendor blog): US average CPI $4.90 in Q1 2026, up 34% in two years; Adjust 2026 gaming report cited for +30% YoY global CPI to $0.56 blended average. GameGrowthAdvisor: average iOS CPI $4.22, Android $2.97; AppLovin iOS $2.80-4.50, Unity $2.50-4.00 (unaudited estimates). Sources disagree on baselines. — [FoxData](https://foxdata.com/en/blogs/mobile-game-ua-in-2026-why-cpi-is-rising-and-what-smart-studios-do-differently/); [GameGrowthAdvisor](https://gamegrowthadvisor.com/blog/2026-03-17-user-acquisition-cpi-benchmarks-2026/)
- Retention: ~27% D1 across genres in 2025 per one blog; another notes genre retention data is stale (last broad public genre breakdown AppsFlyer Q3 2022, median D1 about 22%). — [Segwise](https://segwise.ai/blog/mobile-gaming-app-user-retention-strategies.md); [GameGrowthAdvisor](https://gamegrowthadvisor.com/blog/2026-03-17-mobile-game-kpis-benchmarks-2026/)
- Ad stack (Sensor Tower): video 53.7% of creatives, playables 13.3% (nearly doubled), rewarded 17.7% of impressions (+53.9%); AdMob 33% (down), AppLovin 27.3% (growing), Mintegral 16%; social share Instagram 35.6%, Facebook 33.6%, TikTok 12.7%, YouTube 12%. — [GameDev Reports](https://gamedevreports.substack.com/p/sensor-tower-state-of-gaming-2026)
- Commentary: creators and cross-game collaborations growing as organic levers; custom product pages help conversion. — [Global Games Forum](https://www.globalgamesforum.com/news/predictions-for-mobile-games-in-2026); [FoxData](https://foxdata.com/en/blogs/mobile-game-ua-in-2026-why-cpi-is-rising-and-what-smart-studios-do-differently/)

### Inferences
- Android CPI is roughly 5-10x cheaper than iOS in casual (Liftoff figures), making Android + LATAM/SEA the only plausible paid-test lane; but a low-ARPU region can't pay back even cheap CPIs without ads.
- Organic share of new installs is under pressure from the shrinking share of new releases in charts (2% of top 300), so organic must come from social/community not store charts.

### Gaps
- No LATAM or SEA CPI numbers found; no primary ATT/SKAN or Privacy Sandbox status data; nothing on AppLovin/Unity policy changes for indies; no TikTok/Shorts conversion data for indies; no ASO trend study.

## 4. Platform dynamics (subscriptions, programs, alt stores, Steam)

### Takeaway
Fee structure is loosening materially in 2026 (Epic v. Apple link-outs; Google Epic settlement at 20%), enabling web shops, though the final Apple fee is unresolved. Subscription catalogs look weak/closed for small devs; Steam is a proven second platform for roguelites but indie revenue there is not growing.

### Cited Findings
- Epic v. Apple: April 2025 contempt ruling bars Apple commissions on external-link purchases; Ninth Circuit upheld contempt but allowed some compensation; in August 2026 Apple proposed 15% on external purchases (5% for Small Business Program apps, 10% for certain partner programs); Epic opposes; not final. Supreme Court status conflicting between sources (April 2026 vs August 2026 reports) — verify. — [TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2026/04/06/apple-epic-games-lawsuit-supreme-court-appeal-app-store-commission/); [iPhone in Canada](https://www.iphoneincanada.ca/2026/08/14/apple-proposes-15-cut-purchases-outside-app-store/); [Channel Insider](https://www.channelinsider.com/apple/news-apple-external-purchase-fee-epic-court-fight/)
- Google/Epic settlement (reported March 4, 2026): Google IAP commission reduced to 20% (plus 5% if using Google billing, per summary); "Registered App Stores" program for streamlined alt-store installs, outside US first, court approval needed. — [TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2026/03/04/google-settles-with-epic-games-drops-its-Play-Store-commissions-to-20/)
- Android Developer Verification: rollout starting April 2026; mandatory in Brazil, Indonesia, Singapore, Thailand from September 2026, global 2027 (dates from secondary sources, verify); registration fee $25 and government ID; limited free accounts. Relevant: Brazil is LATAM. — [Cybernews](https://cybernews.com/security/google-test-sideloading-unverified-android-apps/); [Ground News](https://ground.news/article/google-details-new-24-hour-process-to-sideload-unverified-android-apps)
- Web shops: about 74% of top mobile games have one (Bain: up from 12% in 2019); D2C about $17B, roughly 15% of mobile IAP in 2025; one blog says web shop nets about $0.92 vs $0.70 via App Store, third-party providers charge 5-7% (vendor claim). Casual/hypercasual titles stay below 5-8% of revenue via web shops. — [Bain](https://www.bain.com/insights/game-distribution-half-your-players-already-buy-direct-gaming-report-2026/); [WN Hub](https://wnhub.io/news/analytics/item-51815); [FoxData](https://foxdata.com/en/blogs/mobile-game-d2c-revenues-hit-17-billion-why-publishers-are-pushing-beyond-app-stores/); [Xsolla](https://secure.businesswire.com/news/home/20260928312597/en/Xsolla-Releases-The-Xsolla-Report-Mobile-D2C-Edition)
- Apple Arcade: multiple developer reports of cancelled projects, falling payouts, and disillusioned studios, indie narrative/premium titles most exposed (reports mostly older, 2022-ish); service still operating. Balatro launched on Apple Arcade. Google Play Pass rated fine by devs but poorly marketed. No 2026 shutdown reports found for Netflix Games or Play Pass. — [MobileGamer.biz](https://mobilegamer.biz/inside-apple-arcade-axed-games-declining-payouts-disillusioned-studios-and-an-uncertain-future/); [Pocket Tactics](https://www.pockettactics.com/apple-arcade/dying)
- Google Play Indie Games Accelerator exists (past editions 2018, 2021); 2026 status unconfirmed. — [i-programmer](https://i-programmer.info/news/144-graphics-and-games/14619-google-launches-indie-games-accelerator.html)

### Inferences
- The Small Business Program rate (reportedly 15% store commission under $1M; 5% proposed on link-outs) plus Google's 20% settlement means store fees are not the binding constraint for a solo dev; discoverability is.
- Web shops matter mainly for F2P with repeat spenders; for a premium or light-IAP puzzle-roguelite, the 5-8% casual share suggests low priority.
- Steam as second platform: proven for roguelikes (Balatro, Slay the Spire), but Steam indie revenue fell in 2025 and shovelware/AI-clone volume is high.

### Gaps
- Could not verify current Apple Small Business Program (15% under $1M) and Google 15% terms from primary sources; no 2026 Apple Arcade/Netflix Games/Play Pass entry criteria or payout data; no mobile-to-Steam port performance data; Google Play "Indie" program details for 2026 not found.

## 5. Regulation and risk

### Takeaway
2026 enforcement is converging on virtual currencies, loot boxes and child-directed purchase pressure (EU CPC action, forthcoming Digital Fairness Act, Brazil's new child-protection law). A premium or ad-light puzzle game with no loot boxes is largely outside the target zone, but Brazil's law and age-gating deserve a check.

### Cited Findings
- EU: on 30 Sept 2026 the CPC Network launched coordinated action against nine game companies (including King, Mojang, Riot, Supercell, Ubisoft) over virtual currencies, loot boxes, fake timers, and urging children to buy. Commission expected to present the Digital Fairness Act around 11 Nov 2026 (timing conflicts across sources; text not published). Considering default-off or bans of certain design features in child-directed games. — [WKO newsletter, 5 Oct 2026](https://www.wko.at/information-consulting/werbung-marktkommunikation/aig-newsletter-5-october-2026); [CADE Project](https://cadeproject.org/?p=153018); [Makeinfluence](https://www.makeinfluence.com/en/academy/loot-boxes-and-gacha-mechanics-what-the-eus-digital-fairness-act-could-mean-for-creators-who-promote-them)
- Brazil: Law 15,211 (child and adolescent protection in digital environments) entered into force 17 March 2026 with obligations for electronic games including loot boxes; one source says it bans loot box sales to under-18s (wording not verified against the law text). — [Digital Policy Alert](https://digitalpolicyalert.org/event/33580-bill-on-protection-of-children-and-adolescents-in-digital-environments-including-obligations-for-electronic-games-law-no-15211-enters-into-force)
- UK/Denmark: loot boxes not automatically gambling under current law. — [Programming Insider](https://programminginsider.com/loot-boxes-regulation-and-where-the-line-sits-in-2026/)

### Inferences
- Avoid randomized paid mechanics and countdown pressure; this aligns with a deterministic deduction puzzle and lowers compliance risk. Brazil's age-related obligations apply to LATAM launch.

### Gaps
- No verified information on Privacy Sandbox/SKAN 2026 changes, COPPA/GDPR-K updates, EU DMA effects on mobile games, or Apple/Google age-assurance rules; Brazil law details unverified.

## 6. Underserved opportunities (premium/no-ads, accessibility, offline, cross-platform)

### Takeaway
Direct evidence for demand is weak: available surveys are old and show most mobile players prefer free-with-ads over paid. Opportunity signals are indirect (Balatro mobile sales, "no generative AI" differentiation, puzzle as the top ad-revenue genre implying ad fatigue risk).

### Cited Findings
- Older surveys: 86% of 500 iOS users preferred free games with ads over paid (WildTangent/IHS, roughly a decade old); WARC: 54% of 2,000 US mobile players say premium games must be free to play; majority of mobile gamers prefer earning items via skill rather than paying or watching ads (Toluna). — [BGR](https://www.bgr.com/general/in-game-advertisement-survey/); [WARC](https://www.warc.com/content/feed/mobile-gaming-experiences-can-be-premium/8221)
- Balatro mobile (premium, ad-free) strong first-week sales (see section 2).
- Rewarded ad impressions +53.9% YoY and puzzle holding 53% of ad revenue share indicate an ad-saturated category. — [GameDev Reports](https://gamedevreports.substack.com/p/sensor-tower-state-of-gaming-2026); [WN Hub](https://wnhub.io/news/analytics/item-51344)

### Inferences
- A one-time-purchase or "remove ads" model plus offline play and cross-platform progress (mobile + Steam) is a differentiation hypothesis, not a validated mass-market demand; conversion will likely be small, so it needs low UA spend and a hook.
- Accessibility and offline support are cheap to build and help store-feature eligibility; no market data confirms revenue impact.

### Gaps
- No 2025-26 data on premium mobile revenue share, offline play demand, accessibility demand, cross-platform progress, or LATAM willingness to pay for premium; no Sensor Tower/AppMagic premium-app data retrieved.
