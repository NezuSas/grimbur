## 1. What this app is

Wumpus Adventures is a single-player puzzle game. It does not require an account and does not ask for your real name, email, location,
contacts, photos or microphone.

## 3. Legal basis (where applicable)

Consent (you decide whether to submit a score) and our legitimate interest in running the game. For Ecuador this follows the Ley Orgánica de
Protección de Datos Personales (LOPDP); for the EU/UK, the GDPR.

## 4. Sharing

Leaderboard entries are stored with Google Firebase (a processor acting on our behalf). We do not share data with anyone else, except where the law requires.

## 5. Retention and your rights

Leaderboard entries stay until we remove them. You can ask us to delete an entry that contains your display name by writing to [YOUR EMAIL] with the
name and approximate score/date; we will delete it within [30] days. You also have the right to access, rectify and object, subject to the law that applies to you.
Local progress is deleted by clearing the app's data or uninstalling the app.

## 6. Children

The game is not directed to children under 13 (or the age required in your country), and we do not knowingly collect personal data from them.
